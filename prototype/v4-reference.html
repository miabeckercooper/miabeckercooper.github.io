Which version of the strategy survives?

We tested four versions of the Substrate Capital long-short and reported all of them. The deflated Sharpe ratio corrects for having tried several, which is where weak versions get exposed.

How likely is the turnaround case to beat the market price?

For Nike's management case, we ran a Monte Carlo over revenue growth, margins and WACC instead of trusting one point estimate. Run it again and watch the distribution settle.

Rebuilt from the distribution in our report: median $65, P10–P90 $44–$99, about 75% above the $52.40 share price. Dashed lines: share price $52.40 and management base case $69.05.

What does volatility actually look like?

From our derivatives assignment: a stock starting at €100 with 30% expected return and 38% volatility, simulated over five years under geometric Brownian motion.

Volatility σ38%

The mean ends far above the median: a few paths explode and drag the average up. That skew is why option prices care about volatility, and why the "average" outcome isn't the typical one.
